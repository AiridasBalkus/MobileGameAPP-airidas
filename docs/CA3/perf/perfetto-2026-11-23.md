# Perfetto trace: Mino 0.9.1 on the heaviest section

Recorded 25 Nov 2026 (lab dated 23 Nov). Release 0.9.1 (4c05bd73), Galaxy S24 Ultra (SM-S928U1), Android 17, tier High, **High FPS mode (120 fps target)**.
Section: past 400 m, all five chunk types active, near top speed. 10 s trace (`pace.pftrace`), config `mino-trace.pbtx` (sched, CPU frequency and idle, gfx/view/input atrace, SurfaceFlinger frame timeline).

|--------|---------|-----------------|-------|------------------|----------------------|
| 1,189 in 9.89 s (120.2 fps) | **8.32 ms** | **10.29 ms** | 11.93 ms | 0 | 0 (0.0%) |

**Method.** Android's frame timeline only tracks View-based surfaces; it recorded the status bar and the display, not Unity's SurfaceView. Frame time was therefore taken as the interval between successive `dequeueBuffer` calls on Mino's SurfaceView (pid 2200, Unity graphics thread, Vulkan with Swappy frame pacing). Intervals vary 5.4 to 11.9 ms on the CPU side, but none reaches the ~16.7 ms a missed 120 Hz vsync would produce.

**freq track.** Performance cores (CPU 2 to 6) ran 0.5 to 3.15 GHz and reached their 3.15 / 2.96 GHz maximum during the trace; efficiency cores (CPU 0 to 1) 0.56 to 1.80 GHz; prime core (CPU 7) stayed below 0.9 GHz. Clocks reached their ceiling, so the changes are normal load-following, not thermal throttling.

**Reading.** Mino holds 120 fps on its heaviest section with no missed frames and no throttling, matching the Week 7 High FPS soak (8.32 ms average).
