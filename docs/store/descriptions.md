## Long description

Swipe left and right to steer a little diver down a sunlit reef, dodging coral and scooping up gold coins.
Every run is a 1,200 m dash that gets faster the further you go, so each attempt takes about two minutes.
Spend your coins on faster lane changes and come back to beat your best distance.

FEATURES
- Three-lane swipe controls built for one thumb in portrait
- An underwater reef with drifting bubbles, kelp, rocks and coral
- Speed ramps up as you go, and new obstacle patterns appear further down the reef
- Coin lines placed next to hazards: grab them, or play it safe
- Spend coins on lane-change speed upgrades you feel straight away
- Your best score is saved, with a "New best!" moment when you beat it
- Pause any time; the game also pauses itself if you take a call or switch apps
- Options for haptics, sound and text size, plus a 120 fps mode on phones that support it

No account, no ads, no in-app purchases and no internet needed. No data leaves your phone; your progress is saved locally.
